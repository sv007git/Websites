# InviteDecide — User & Organizer Guide

Welcome to InviteDecide — the intelligent event invitation, RSVP, catering, seating, and check-in platform.

---

## 1. Creating Your First Event
1. Navigate to **Events > New Event**.
2. Fill in Event Details: Name, Category (Wedding, Birthday, Corporate), Host Names, Venue, Address, and Dates.
3. Configure Multi-day Functions (e.g. Mehendi, Sangeet, Wedding Ceremony, Reception).
4. Save and open the Event Dashboard.

---

## 2. Designing Digital Invitations
1. Open the **Designer** tab.
2. Select an elegant starting template (e.g. Royal Indian Wedding, Modern South Indian, Floral Elegance, Luxury Gold).
3. Use the visual editor to customize headers, venue details, colors, fonts, and decorative borders.
4. Preview on Desktop and Mobile viewport sizes.
5. Click **Publish Invitation** to activate live links for your guests.

---

## 3. Managing Guests & Families
1. Go to **Guests**.
2. Add guests individually or click **Import CSV / Excel** to upload your guest list.
3. Group related guests into Families (e.g., "The Sharma Family").
4. Specify adult vs. child classification, dietary habits, and VIP tags.
5. Click **Generate Unique Links** to create cryptographically secure invitation tokens for each family.

---

## 4. Sending WhatsApp Invitations
1. Under **WhatsApp**, review personalized messages.
2. Filter by guest category, RSVP status, or function.
3. For automated sending, connect your Meta WhatsApp Cloud API credentials in Organization Settings.
4. For instant sending, click the **WhatsApp** icon next to any guest to open WhatsApp Web/Mobile with their prefilled personalized invitation and link.

---

## 5. Guest RSVP Experience
1. Guest opens their unique link: `#/invite/<token>`.
2. Views the interactive digital invitation card.
3. Clicks **RSVP Now**.
4. Confirms who is attending (adults & children).
5. Selects their preferred arrival time slot.
6. Chooses meal selections from your menu for each attending member.
7. Submits and immediately receives their **Individual or Family QR Pass**.

---

## 6. Catering Calculation Engine
1. Go to **Catering** in the organizer dashboard.
2. Set your preferred safety buffer percentage (default 10%).
3. The platform computes exact dish-level quantities based on confirmed RSVPs.
4. Export the Catering Order Sheet to PDF or Excel for your caterer.

---

## 7. Venue Seating Plan
1. Open the **Seating** module.
2. Add tables (Round, Rectangular, Square, Rows) and configure seat capacities.
3. Click **Auto-Allocate Seating** to assign families together with VIP priority.
4. Drag and drop any seat or guest to fine-tune assignments manually.

---

## 8. Event-Day Check-in & QR Scanning
1. Gate staff navigate to **Staff Check-in** on their mobile phone.
2. Point the camera at the guest's QR Pass.
3. System validates the token, displays attendee names, verifies seat assignments, and marks attendance atomically.
4. Monitor live attendance and remaining guests on the Live Check-in Dashboard.
