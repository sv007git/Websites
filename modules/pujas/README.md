## 🌟 Key Features

### 1. 🌸 Neatly Ordered by Phase of Life (షోడశ సంస్కారాలు)
Step-by-step Vedic rituals celebrating the milestones of life from womb to elderhood:
- **Seemantham (Simantonnayana)**: 6th–8th month pregnancy ritual for maternal tranquility and child's sharp intellect.
- **Namakaranam (Naming Ceremony)**: Whispering astrological and popular names into newborn's ear.
- **Annaprashana**: 6th month first solid sweet rice feeding with gold ring & destiny choice test.
- **Aksharabhyasa (Vidyarambha)**: Saraswati & Hayagreeva initiation, tracing 'OM' on raw rice.
- **Upanayanam (Vatuvugu)**: Sacred thread (Yajnopaveetam) investiture, Brahmopadesham (Gayatri Mantra), and Bhavati Bhiksham Dehi.
- **Vivaha Samskara**: Sacred Vedic wedding, Jeelakarra-Bellam, Kanyadanam, Mangalasutradharana, and Saptapadi (seven sacred vows).
- **Griha Pravesham**: Cow (Go) entry, milk boiling, threshold dwara puja, and Vastu Shanti.
- **Shashtiabdapoorthi (60th Birthday)**: Ugraratha Shanti, Ayushya & Maha Mrityunjaya Homa, Sahasradhara herbal bath, and renewal of marriage vows.
- **Pitru Karyam & Shraddha**: Ancestral gratitude, Tila Tarpana, Pinda Dana, and Kaka/Go bali.

---

### 2. 🪔 Vratams & Major Festivals (వ్రతాలు మరియు పండుగలు)
- **Sri Satyanarayana Swamy Vratam**: Navagraha mandala worship, 5 chapters of sacred katha, Sapada bhakshya prasadam.
- **Sri Varalakshmi Vratam**: Sravana Sukravara nomu, 9-knot yellow Toram consecration, Amma mukham alankaram, and sumangali tamboolam.
- **Vinayaka Chavithi (Ganesha Chaturthi)**: 21 medicinal botanical leaves (Ekavimshati Patra Puja), Modaka naivedyam, Shamantakopakhyana katha.
- **Maha Shivaratri**: Panchamrita Lingabhishekam, Bilvashtaka archana, Rudram chanting, and all-night vigil (Jagaran).
- **Deepavali Lakshmi-Kubera Puja**: Diya illumination, gold/silver coin archana, Kubera Pashupata mantra, and business ledger puja.

---

### 3. 🔱 Dosha Pariharams & Shantis (దోష పరిహారాలు)
- **Navagraha Shanti & Peeda Pariharam**: Remedies for Shani Sade Sati, Ashtama Shani, Rahu-Ketu transit, and 9-grain donation.
- **Rahu-Ketu & Sarpa Dosha Nivarana**: For Kala Sarpa Dosha, Naga Dosha, marriage delays, milk abhishekam, and Subrahmanya/Naga Gayatri.
- **Kuja Dosha (Manglik) Pariharam**: Subrahmanya Shashti archana, red lentils (Kandulu) donation in red cloth, and Angaraka Stotram.
- **Santana Gopala Swamy Puja**: For couples desiring virtuous children; Balakrishna cradle worship with fresh butter and 108 Moola Mantra japa.
- **Balarishta & Dristi (Evil Eye) Dosha Nivarana**: Sea salt, dry red chili waving, and Hanuman Kavacha raksha thread.

---

### 4. ☀️ Daily Observances (నిత్య పూజలు)
- **Nitya Deeparadhana & Griha Deva Puja**: Morning and evening twilight lamp lighting, Achamanam, Ghanta Nada, and Panchopachara offering.
- **Shiva Pradosha Vrata Puja**: Trayodashi evening twilight abhishekam to Lord Shiva and Nandi for relief from debts and health issues.

## 🔊 Audio Recitation & Sacred Sound Effects
- **Web Speech API**: Speaks entire pujas hands-free (Priest Mode) or individual mantras and steps. Supports native **Telugu (`te-IN`)** and **English (`en-IN` / `en-US`)**.
- **Synthesized Temple Bell (Ghanta Dhwani)**: Real-time physical acoustic modeling via Web Audio API generating authentic resonance (850Hz, 1200Hz, 1800Hz harmonics) without external MP3 files.
- **Sacred Conch (Shankham)**: Low-frequency resonant drone sound.
- **Playback Controls**: Play, Pause, Resume, Stop, Next Step, Previous Step, and Speed (0.75x meditative, 0.9x, 1.0x, 1.2x).

---

## 🛠️ High-Value Interactive Utilities
- **🌐 Bilingual Toggle**: Instant 1-click switch between **తెలుగు (Telugu)** and **English**. All titles, mantras, samagri lists, steps, and meanings update instantly.
- **🌓 Dark & White Themes**:
  - *Dark Theme (Temple Night)*: Charcoal black, warm saffron, marigold gold, glowing diya amber.
  - *Light Theme (Vedic Ashram Marble)*: Pristine ivory, deep kumkuma red, turmeric gold, crisp sacred typography.
- **✨ Personalized Vedic Sankalpam Builder**:
  - Inputs for Gotram, Janma Nakshatram, Name, Spouse Name.
  - Dynamically calculates current **Samvatsara, Ayana, Ritu, Masa, Paksha, Tithi, Vasara**.
  - Outputs full Sanskrit text, English transliteration, Telugu & English meanings, and includes an audio speech button!
- **🧺 Interactive Samagri Checklist**: Check off items as you buy/prepare them, view progress bar (e.g. `12 / 15 gathered`), reset checklist, or export directly to **WhatsApp**!
- **🛡️ Screen Wake Lock API**: Keeps your mobile/tablet screen awake throughout the entire puja so you don't have to touch your device with wet hands!

## 🚀 How to Publish to GitHub Pages

1. **Verify your Git Remote**:
   ```bash
   git remote -v
   # Should point to: https://github.com/sv007git/Pujas.git
   ```

2. **Commit and Push to GitHub**:
   ```bash
   git add .
   git commit -m "Initial commit: Hindu Pujas & Pariharams with audio, life phases, themes, and sankalpam builder"
   git push -u origin main
   ```

3. **Enable GitHub Pages on GitHub**:
   - Go to your repository: [https://github.com/sv007git/Pujas](https://github.com/sv007git/Pujas)
   - Click on **Settings** > **Pages** (in the left sidebar).
   - Under **Build and deployment**:
     - **Source**: Select `Deploy from a branch`
     - **Branch**: Select `main` and folder `/ (root)`
     - Click **Save**.
   - Alternatively, the included GitHub Actions workflow (`.github/workflows/deploy.yml`) will automatically deploy it for you!

4. **Access your site**:
   Your hosted application will be live at:
   👉 **`https://sv007git.github.io/Pujas/`**
